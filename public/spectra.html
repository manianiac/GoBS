Ethical Necromancy & Thanatological Stewardship

The Corpus Corpus Corpus & Collegium

Under the direction of Chairman Toland, the Guild of the Black Sky has pioneered using the undead as industrial power tools and machinery. We operate in strict compliance with the code of regulations around magical education and necromancy.

Industrial Workforce

Undead as Industrial Machinery

Toland pioneered the application of reanimated skeletons as tireless, non-skilled power tools. Skeletal units perform demanding physical labor—such as digging, subterranean excavation, and carrying crushing loads up mountain terraces where carts and pack animals would be physically infeasible.

  • Living Custodian Oversight: All undead laborers work under the constant direct supervision of certified living custodians.
  • Zero Biological Fatigue: Uninterrupted continuous shifts in hazardous, unventilated subterranean shafts and steep vertical cliffs.
  • Respectful Maintenance: Skeletons are treated with rigorous professional care, cleaned in alchemical osteo-baths, and maintained with specialized bone wax.
Training Facility

Corpus Corpus Collegium

Our premier Ethical Necromancy Training Facility. Here, matriculating students learn the disciplined, civic science of thanatology in full compliance with realm laws on magical education.

  • Workforce Management: Training students to effectively direct and manage large cadres of skeletal power tools safely.
  • Osteo-Alchemical Maintenance: Rigorous lab instruction on cleaning, repairing, and preserving skeletal remains.
  • Advanced Reanimation: For gifted students, structured curriculum on ethically raising the dead without infringing on departed souls.
