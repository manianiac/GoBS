Executive Leadership & Roster

Founded in May 2018, the Guild of the Black Sky operates as a benevolent corptocracy. Decisions outside of combat are decided democratically by the Council of Chairmen with equal voting for each department, while advancement is earned strictly through demonstrated merit, ability, and civic service.
