Aerosystems Division // Confluence Skyport

Rigid-Hull Airship Fleet Architecture

Lighter-than-air flight in the Guild began when Owain Fairwind piloted our legendary experimental dire seal air balloon, proving the technology worked. Today, the Guild engineers the realm's premier rigid-hull airships, combining pressurized phlogiston lifting envelopes with reinforced iron-oak frames.

Tactical Aviation Doctrine

High-Altitude Dominance & Dropped Payloads

Because black powder does not function in the physical laws of Osterra, our airships operate with uncompromising kinetic ingenuity. Flying above the range of ground archers, our vessels project power through heavy torsion ballistae and devastating dropped payloads.

  • Heavy Torsion Ballistae: Precision broadside and dorsal ballista mounts firing armor-piercing steel bolts at aerial raiders and wyverns.
  • Shaped Kinetic Masses: Specially shaped metal penetrators and dense stone masses dropped from high altitude that inflict catastrophic impact damage upon fortified encampments.
  • Airborne Skeletal Drops: Rapid stratospheric release of non-sentient skeletal labor and combat cadres deployed directly into contested areas or unventilated work sites.
