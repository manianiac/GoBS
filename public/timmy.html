LITTLE TIMMY’S STAND

Civic Sentinel Gatehouse // Confluence Southern Gate

“Dedicated in eternal honor of Lil’ Timmy, who stood his ground when the earth broke open and Pirate Prince Rory breached our peaceful grounds.

Though mortal breath failed, courage never faltered. Where he stood, an unbroken gatehouse now bars the subterranean dark. May his memory remind every citizen of Confluence that our walls are only as strong as the spirits who protect them.”
