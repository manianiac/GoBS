Bureau of Regulatory Affairs & Sacred Desk Registers

Canonical GBSF Forms Repository

Welcome to the central bureaucratic repository of the Guild of the Black Sky. In accordance with Confluence municipal code and the codified regulations of Osterra, all agreements, member contracts, corpse pledges, requisitions, and grievances must be formally filed in the Arcane Ledgers.

GMSF0A PREREQUISITE DOCKET

Petition for Form

The foundational administrative instrument of Black Sky bureaucracy. Required by all citizens, contractors, and visiting envoys prior to receiving permission to request any subsequent specialized GBSF docket.

AGENCY: SACRED DESK REGISTRY WOODFORD AUDIT: REQUIRED
GBSF1A CAREER TRACK

Permanent Member Contract

Official lifetime membership indenture. Enrolls the candidate under the Council of Chairmen with full voting franchise, guarantees quarterly seasonal salary based on highest tier, and guarantees legal defense by Black Sky shield legions.

AGENCY: EXECUTIVE COUNCIL DISBURSEMENT: 3-MONTH CYCLE
GBSF2D

Temporary Contractor Agreement

Term agreement for external specialists, alchemists, carpenters, and mercenary squads contracted for specific seasonal campaigns or construction initiatives in the Confluence Terraces.

AGENCY: OPERATIONS & LOGISTICS DURATION: 1–3 MONTHS
GBSF3C

Official Requisition Form

Official docket to requisition raw materials, glowstone bricks, phlogiston fuel cells, weapons, armor, or alchemical silver from the Central Confluence Arsenals.

AGENCY: INFRASTRUCTURE (RIASTRAD) CENTRAL ARSENAL SECTOR
GBSF4A

Permanent Contract Amendments

Allows senior guild members to formally request adjustments to duty stations, salary tier recognition, departmental transfers, or leaves of absence.

AGENCY: SACRED DESK LEGAL REVIEW: COUNCIL OF CHAIRMEN
GBSF5A

Temporary Faction Member Contract

Accelerated auxiliary contract designed for traveling Everwar adventurers and allied foreign citizens seeking temporary asylum, wages, or mutual defense within Black Sky borders.

AGENCY: INTERNAL HARMONY (NIKOS) ONBOARDING: EXPEDITED
GBSF6B

Standard Delivery Catalogue

The codified pricing roster of standard timber, iron ingots, grain supplies, slow-smoked provisions from The Butcher, and glowstone gravel available for export across Osterra.

AGENCY: TRADE & COMMERCE UPDATED: QUARTERLY
GBSF7B

Catalogue Bill of Receipt

Mandatory dockside signoff verifying cargo arrival, inspectoral seals, and freight tonnage delivered to the Confluence Sky Docks or subterranean freight gates.

AGENCY: SKYPORT CUSTOMS BERTHS 1–3 DOCKSIDE
GBSF8A CHURCH OF PECUNE

Official Bilateral Trade Treaty

Sacred commercial pact ratified under Pecune's blessing. Legally binds tariffs, exchange rates, and safe-conduct guarantees between Confluence and foreign realms (such as The Grove or White Ravens).

AGENCY: TRADE & FINANCES JURISDICTION: INTER-FACTION
GBSF9A

Official Certified Invoice

Certified bill of indebtedness demanding payment in gold sovereigns, silver crowns, or certified mithril notes. Issued with strict payment windows and late fees.

AGENCY: EXCHEQUER'S VAULTS REPUTATION TOKENS ACCEPTED
GBSF10A HIGH MARKET

Premium Delivery Catalogue

Exclusive registry of high-tier enchanted weapons, refined mithril bullion, ancient Magelord relics, and customized airship parts available in Confluence's High Market.

AGENCY: HIGH MARKET COMMERCE CURRENCY: MITHRIL & GOLD
GBSF12A HIGH COURT

Petition for Redress of Grievances (Libel, Defamation, Slander)

Formal legal filing submitted to the High Court of Confluence to seek monetary damages or public retraction for false statements, defamation of professional character, or breach of business respect.

AGENCY: HIGH COURT OMBUDSMAN REVIEW: EXPEDITED
GBSF13A BAZAAR STALL LICENSE

Independent Merchant Contract

Commercial license permitting independent merchants and foreign trading companies to erect vendor stalls in the High Market, granting access to Confluence's bustling foot traffic and guard protection.

AGENCY: HIGH MARKET BAZAAR DISTRICT: HIGH MARKET
GBSF14A / 15A

Indication of Diplomatic Interest (Successful / Failed)

Official diplomatic memorandum recording inter-faction summits. GBSF14A ratifies border treaties and alliance terms; GBSF15A archives failed negotiations for future legal and tactical precedent.

AGENCY: FOREIGN AFFAIRS COUNCIL RECORDED BY WOODFORD
GBSF702B ETHICAL NECROMANCY

Voluntary Post-Mortem Power-Tool Asset Pledge

Enables a citizen to voluntarily pledge their physical vessel to the Corpus Corpus Corpus program (§ 000.10) for non-sentient somatic reflex power-tool labor (deep excavation, heavy hauling where carts are infeasible) under living custodian supervision, adhering strictly to the Ethical Necromancy Code of Regulations.

AGENCY: CORPUS CORPUS COLLEGIUM SUPERVISION: LIVING CUSTODIANS
GBSF33B REGULATION § 301.3

Insignia Calibration & Corporate Stitching Audit

Mandatory audit under Guild Regulation § 301.3. Requires all officers, merchants, and contractors to submit field insignias and uniforms for dimensional calibration to prevent fraudulent corporate representation.

AGENCY: COMPLIANCE & PROTOCOL ANNUAL AUDIT CYCLE
GBSF101D REGULATION § 101.4

Petition for Name Exemption: Prohibition on the Use of the Name David

Formal petition filed with the Council to request a temporary waiver for visiting dignitaries, merchant sailors, or reanimated skeletal assets who possess the restricted name "David."

AGENCY: COUNCIL OF COUNSELS RESTRICTED NOMENCLATURE
GBSF1001H REGULATION § 1001.7

Emergency Distress Manifest ("Please Send Help")

Official expedited emergency docket filed in the event of catastrophic dimensional rift rupture, psychic breach from He Who Eats, or siege by anomalous subterranean entities. Automatically alerts Sir Garon Ironrock's spear-wall division.

AGENCY: DNEC (§ 000.9) & OPERATIONS EMERGENCY DISPATCH
