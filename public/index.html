Corporate Charter & Philosophy

Why Join The Guild of the Black Sky?

We are a fantasy megacorp and merchant coalition operating as a benevolent corptocracy. The seriousness of our corporate structure is our trademark: exact bylaws, disciplined warfare, and unwavering protection for our people.

Governance

Equal Departmental Democracy

No single autocrat rules Confluence. Decisions outside of active battlefield combat are resolved by equal voting among the Council of Chairmen. Promotions are granted strictly on demonstrated merit, competence, and service.

Guaranteed Compensation

Consistent Quarterly Salaries

Few factions in Osterra offer reliable financial stability. Black Sky members receive guaranteed seasonal salaries every three months based on their highest attained tier, managed by the Church of Pecune.

Martial Doctrine

Disciplined Team Combat

We don't fight as disorganized zealots. Squads reach consensus on mission objectives, anchor the front with tower shields and heavy AP3 spears, maneuver skirmishers and mages on the flanks, and outlast opponents with superior defenses.

Contractual Integrity

Fair Rules, Zero Malicious Traps

Our legal codes and paperwork are exacting, but strictly fair. We actively avoid hidden pitfalls and gotchas. Operate within Black Sky bylaws, and the full weight of our airships, legions, and treasury will stand in your defense.
